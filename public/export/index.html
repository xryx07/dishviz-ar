About DishViz

DishViz is an AR menu platform that helps restaurants show their dishes in 3D before guests order. Instead of guessing from text or low-quality photos, customers can scan a QR code, rotate the dish, see portions clearly, and order with confidence.

We're building DishViz to:

  • • Reduce confusion and disappointments at the table
  • • Help restaurants increase average order value
  • • Create a modern, memorable dining experience

Started in 2025, DishViz is currently working with early pilot restaurants to bring "see before you order" to menus across India and beyond.
